I am a master's student at **Harbin Institute of Technology**, working in the **Sentiment Computing group at SCIR** with [Prof. Yanyan Zhao](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/yanyan?lang=zh) and [Prof. Weixiang Zhao](https://circle-hit.github.io/). I am also a research intern at **Huawei Consumer Business Group**.

My research explores how agents **remember users over time, retrieve relevant context, and decide when and how to use memory**. I also work on personalized and empathetic dialogue and language model safety.
